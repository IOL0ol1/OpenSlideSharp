OpenSlide Viewer (OpenSlideSharp.Viewer): third-party notices
=============================================================

The viewer is part of OpenSlideSharp (https://github.com/IOL0ol1/OpenSlideSharp), which is MIT licensed; its
license is in OpenSlideSharp/LICENSE next to this file. The viewer's executable also contains the components
below, each under its own license.

Where a component's NuGet package ships license or notice files, the publish copies them into a folder named
after the package, next to this file. The licenses of the other components are reproduced at the end of this
file. OpenSlideSharp.runtime's LICENSE.txt is left out: it describes the layout of that package, and item 1 and
SOURCE.md say the same for this folder.


1. OpenSlide and the libraries linked into it (openslide-bin)
   License:  OpenSlide: LGPL-2.1-only. The libraries statically linked into it (cairo, glib, libdicom, libffi,
             libjpeg-turbo, libpng, libtiff, libxml2, OpenJPEG, pixman, proxy-libintl, SQLite, uthash, zlib-ng,
             Zstandard, as listed in each platform's VERSIONS.md) are each under their own license.
   Upstream: https://openslide.org/
             https://github.com/openslide/openslide-bin
   Files:    openslide-bin/<platform>/VERSIONS.md (the components and their versions)
             openslide-bin/<platform>/<component>/ (their license texts)
             openslide-bin/openslide-bin.json (the openslide-bin build and its source tarball)
             <platform> is the openslide-bin build of the executable's platform: windows-x64,
             linux-x86_64, linux-aarch64 or macos-arm64-x86_64.
   Source code (the exact build, its source tarball with its sha256, and where to download it), and
   how to use another OpenSlide build instead: SOURCE.md.
